This folder houses all SMILES strings used during the study. The filenames are structured as {Receptor}_{Training then testing or validation}_SMILES.txt and as such, each receptor has two files. {Receptor}_TT_SMILES.txt has the SMILES for the training and testing of the MLPC, and the {Receptor}_V_SMILES.txt file catalogues the validation structures. This folder is meant as a "back-up" in case any SMILES files are missing from the datasets.
